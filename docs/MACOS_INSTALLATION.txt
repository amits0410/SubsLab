INSTALL SUBSLAB ON YOUR MAC

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
Intel Macs are not supported. Python, Homebrew, and developer tools are not needed.

1. In this window, drag the SubsLab app onto the Applications shortcut.
2. Wait for the copy to finish, then eject the SubsLab disk image in Finder.
3. Open Finder > Applications > SubsLab.

FIRST LAUNCH

This release is not signed with Apple Developer ID or notarized by Apple.
If macOS says the developer cannot be verified or Apple cannot check the app:

- Only continue if you expected this download and trust its source.
- Try opening SubsLab once, then open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
- Under Security, click Open Anyway for SubsLab, then confirm Open.

Apple's instructions: https://support.apple.com/102445
If macOS says the app is damaged or will damage your computer, stop and request
a fresh copy. Do not disable macOS security protections.

DOWNLOAD YOUR AI MODEL ONCE

Open Tools > Local AI models and install the model for your language and task.
Hebrew transcription uses Ivrit-AI. Translation into English uses the general
multilingual model. Keep the app open until download and verification finish.
Model installation needs internet; installed models work offline afterward.

CREATE SUBTITLES

Open media, choose the language and transcription/translation mode, and select
Generate subtitles. Review the results and save an SRT subtitle file.

Your audio and video remain on your computer.

Download updates: https://github.com/amits0410/SubsLab/releases/latest
Full guide: https://github.com/amits0410/SubsLab/blob/main/docs/MACOS_INSTALLATION.md
